Clinch Bluff

Twenty home sites off one gated road, on the last lakefront land in Hardin Valley.

We buy 44.45 acres on a bluff above Melton Hill Lake, build one gated road with power, water and internet to every lot, and sell twenty lots over twelve months. Septic and the electric run to each house are paid as each lot sells.

The plot

Twenty home sites off one gated road

Riverfront Lake View Regular

Layout traced from the boundary survey. The civil engineer sets the final lot lines.

One gate, one road

A gated entry on Williams Bend Rd and one private road, about 1,700 ft, from the gate down to the bluff, with a lane along the top of the bluff and an overlook where they meet.

Riverfront   lots on the bluff

About 2.5 acres each, along roughly 1,200 ft of bluff above protected TVA shoreline.

Lake View   lots on the upper slopes

About 2 acres each, on the slopes and ridge, sited for the lake and mountain views.

Regular   wooded lots near the gate

About 2 acres each, private and wooded, the most accessible price.

Every lot ready to build

Paved road, power, water and internet to every lot. Septic and the electric run to the house go in when the lot sells.

Where it is

On the Clinch River arm of Melton Hill Lake

The tract runs along the river side of Williams Bend Rd, in Hardin Valley, about twenty minutes from West Knoxville.

Tract outline from Knox County GIS (KGIS), tax map 102, parcel 011.03. Switch between map and satellite at top right.

Hardin Valley schools

Hardin Valley Elementary 1.8 miles, Hardin Valley Middle 1.9, Hardin Valley Academy 2.0.

On the water

The public boat ramp at Melton Hill Park is about 1.3 miles away, and a marina about 3.3 miles.

The address

0 Williams Bend Rd, Knoxville, TN 37932. Zoned A (Agricultural); twenty lots are allowed by right.

Utilities

Water from West Knox Utility District; power and fiber from LCUB. No public sewer, so each lot gets its own septic system.

The ground

From the road to the water

Schematic from the boundary survey and known site elevations. The topo survey and the engineer’s road profile will replace it.

Preliminary soil map, August 2020. Click to open full size.

Tested in 2020 at three spots

Two home sites and a clubhouse site. The soil perks at 45 to 75 minutes per inch, inside Tennessee’s limit for a standard septic system, with more than 6 ft to rock on the bluff.

The contract waits on a full soil report

Our offer is contingent on a broader soil report across every lot, confirming where each house and septic system can go.
